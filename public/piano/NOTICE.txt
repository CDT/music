The files in this folder are the minor-third note set of that library,
converted to MP3, as redistributed in https://github.com/Tonejs/audio.
They are unmodified apart from that format conversion.

This app downloads them only when you ask it to. Its default instrument is
synthesized in the browser and needs no download at all.
